About

Learn how it works.

Spend Horizon is a budgeting application that helps you plan for what is next.

Track your income and expenses, understand where your money goes, and create savings goals for future purchases.

What you can do

  • Review your monthly financial summary.
  • Organize expenses by category.
  • Create goals and track progress.
  • Estimate how long it will take to reach a goal.
